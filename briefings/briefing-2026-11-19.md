## 🔬 Research

### Jeveration: System One Models Now Generate
**74 upvotes** · Yuhe Liu, Qi Li, Shihua Zhang, Xinchao Wang · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.24738)

Jeveration repurposes a frozen vision-language model (a "System One"-style parallel predictor) for high-quality image generation in a **single forward pass**. The key trick is conditioning all decoding positions on a shared random state to enforce global coherence without iterative denoising. The result: a 512×512 image in **78 ms**, up to **122× faster** than existing unified models, while also supporting image-to-image and dense prediction from the same checkpoint. For engineers building latency-sensitive vision pipelines, this single-pass paradigm is a significant architectural shift worth watching.

### The Resolution Hypothesis: Agent Evolution's Key Is Identifying Improvements
**30 upvotes** · Yuanhao Zeng et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.23068)

Across **7 evolution methods, 6 models, and 10M+ LLM calls**, the authors argue that self-improving agent loops stall not from bad proposals but from **unreliable evaluation**: selection between candidate artifacts (prompts, skills, memories) performs no better than random, and published method rankings hinge on run-to-run variance rather than genuine superiority. This is a sobering finding for anyone building auto-improving agents — the bottleneck isn't the proposer, it's signal quality. The paper calls for the community to shift focus from novel proposal mechanisms to making true improvements reliably detectable.

### ALIVE: Making Streaming Video Models Interactive
**28 upvotes** · Jinzhao Li et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.21366)

ALIVE extends streaming video LLMs beyond passive observation by adding **proactive monitoring, task coordination, and feedback-seeking** in a unified model. Training uses 288K SFT examples plus 3K RL examples, with a novel Behavior-Aligned Credit Assignment (BACA) reward that scores both individual turns and full trajectories. It also ships **FSI-Bench**, a new benchmark for evaluating whether a model knows when to ask for clarification before acting. ALIVE tops four public benchmarks and FSI-Bench, making it a strong baseline for anyone building real-time video agents.

### Quoting Daniel Stenberg — AI curl rewrites miss the moving target
2026-11-18 · [Post](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/18/daniel-stenberg/) · tags: curl, rust, coding-agents, security, vibe-porting

curl's creator notes that while AI agents were busy converting **185,000 lines of C into 340,000 lines of Rust**, the actual curl project merged **300+ commits including ~200 bugfixes and several security patches** — none of which the AI-generated Rust clones have. It's a sharp illustration of a core problem with large-scale "vibe-porting" projects: the source isn't a static artifact, and AI rewrites can fall behind the moment they're created. A timely reminder when evaluating AI-driven code migration proposals.
