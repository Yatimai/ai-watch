## 🔬 Research

### AuK: An Open-Source Foundational Model for Speech Generation and Editing
**187 upvotes** · Ziyang Ma et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.08936)

AuK is a fully open-source audio foundation model trained on ~3.03 billion instruction–audio pairs and 1.95 million hours of supervision, covering speech generation, content/paralinguistic/acoustic editing, and enhancement. It combines a multimodal LLM for semantic conditioning, a joint speech/audio/music VAE, and a hybrid rectified-flow Transformer (MMDiT + DiT). The distilled variant AuK-Flash delivers 4-step inference with a 4.5× wall-clock speedup, making deployment far more practical. Weights and code are fully released, which is notably rare for a system of this breadth.

### OpenWAM: Open, Modular Exploration Towards Systematic World-Action Model Pretraining
**59 upvotes** · Yuran Wang et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.07398)

OpenWAM decomposes the World-Action Model (WAM) design space into composable modules to systematically study what actually makes video-generative priors useful for robot control. Three empirical principles emerge: capable generative backbone + compact latent space, dedicated action capacity with explicit world-to-action flow, and one-stage co-training on egocentric + robot data. The resulting OpenWAM-α, pretrained on ~6,400 hours of data, achieves top-tier results across 8 simulation benchmarks and real-world robot experiments spanning single-arm, bimanual, and dexterous-hand embodiments. Full stack — infrastructure, models, data recipes — is released openly.

### Show-Harness: Just a VLM Agent Can Play Robots
**50 upvotes** · Yanzhe Chen et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.10522)

Show-Harness proposes a thin "embodied harness" that exposes discrete semantic action units to a standard VLM, while embodiment-specific interpreters handle the physical grounding deterministically — no custom robot model needed. This allows closed-source frontier VLMs (e.g. GPT-4o class) to do zero-shot robot control, and small open-source VLMs to be fine-tuned for cheap deployment in just a few GPU-hours. The companion GUMI interface enables humans and agents to collect robot demonstrations via GUI, removing the need for specialized teleoperation hardware. Results outperform representative agentic and Vision-Language-Action (VLA) paradigms across embodiments and environments.

## 🛠 Tools

### ayghri/i-have-adhd — Force your coding agent to lead with the answer
**4,650 stars/day** · 36,567 total stars · [Repo](https://github.com/ayghri/i-have-adhd)

A one-line installable "skill" for coding agents (Claude, Codex, etc.) that restructures outputs to put the action first, number every step, and drop filler phrases like "Hope this helps!" — essentially enforcing terse, scannable responses. It works by instructing the agent via `AGENTS.md` conventions, requiring no code changes to your project. The virality (36k+ stars, trending today) reflects widespread frustration with verbose, hedge-laden LLM outputs in agentic workflows.

### cathrynlavery/diagram-design — 39 editorial diagram types for AI coding agents
**2,249 stars/day** · 37,265 total stars · [Repo](https://github.com/cathrynlavery/diagram-design)

A skill/prompt library providing 39 self-contained HTML+SVG diagram templates — Sankey, Wardley map, UML class, fishbone, dependency graph, and more — designed for Claude Code, Codex, and similar agents. Output is static HTML by default (no Figma, no Mermaid), with optional motion for ordered explanations. Version 2.5.10 added 10 new layout grammars. It also converts existing draw.io, Mermaid, or Excalidraw diagrams to the chosen format at a specified detail level.

### THU-MAIC/OpenMAIC — Multi-agent interactive classroom in one click
**806 stars/day** · 34,915 total stars · [Repo](https://github.com/THU-MAIC/OpenMAIC)

OpenMAIC (Tsinghua University) is an open-source, TypeScript/Next.js system where multiple LLM agents play distinct classroom roles (teacher, tutor, peer student, etc.) to deliver immersive, interactive learning sessions. It supports OpenAI and Anthropic API keys, deploys directly to Vercel, and integrates with local AI via Lemonade. Backed by a JCST 2026 paper, it's a concrete research artifact for studying multi-agent pedagogy, not just a demo.

## 📡 Analysis

### WeWorm: The First Zero-Click AI-Assisted Worm via WeChat Calls
2026-09-10 · [Post](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/10/calif-research/) · tags: `ai-security-research`, `ai`, `llms`, `security`, `generative-ai`

Calif Research published a demo of WeWorm, a zero-click worm that spreads across iOS and Android via WeChat calls — the victim doesn't need to answer or touch their phone. The critical detail for AI engineers: their team used AI to find the bug and write the first RCE exploit in ~2 days, then built the full worm in one more week — a task that previously required larger teams and months of work. This concretely illustrates that AI is now a meaningful force multiplier for offensive security, lowering both the time and expertise barrier significantly.

### Terence Tao on AI and the Scarcity of Open Mathematical Problems
2026-09-09 · [Post](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/9/terence-tao/) · tags: `ai-ethics`, `mathematics`, `ai`

Fields Medalist Terence Tao warns that AI is now "strip-mining" the finite reserve of good open mathematical problems at an unsustainable pace — and that even the rumor of someone working on a problem triggers a flood of AI-powered efforts to solve it first. This creates pressure to stop sharing promising research directions publicly, potentially reversing centuries of open science norms. For AI researchers, this is a direct signal that norms around research transparency are under acute strain in mathematically-adjacent fields, with likely spillover into ML itself.
