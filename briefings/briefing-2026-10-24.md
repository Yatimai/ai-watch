### WeVisOne-VL: Unified Visual Perception from Objects to Pixels
**229 upvotes** · Tianming Liang, Junyi Pan, Yuxi Li et al. (WeChat Vision) · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.31329)

WeVisOne-VL is a VLM from WeChat Vision that handles the full stack of visual perception — detection, segmentation, OCR, counting, matting, and referring localization — within a single next-token prediction framework. A lightweight pixel decoder is injected via a special `<OBJ>` token, enabling instance-level pixel outputs without a separate segmentation head. Trained with a three-stage curriculum SFT + mixed-task RL, it leads on 30+ benchmarks across both object-level and pixel-level tasks. The open-source release is notable: production-grade fine-grained perception from a major platform player entering the public research ecosystem.

### Making MLLM Latents Matter: FAPL for Cinematic Video Generation
**165 upvotes** · Songlin Yang, Zhe Wang et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.30926)

Video Diffusion Transformers tend to ignore MLLM representations by routing around them through native conditioning, making LLM priors effectively useless for cinematic generation. Factor-Aware Predictive Learning (FAPL) fixes this with a three-stage training protocol: forcing the DiT to depend on MLLM latents, teaching it factor-specific control (character, scene, action, camera) via paired video contrasts, and then freezing the DiT to backpropagate through it into the MLLM for temporal inference. Results show consistent gains on sparse-to-video, factor-aware editing, and multi-shot generation tasks — a meaningful step toward controllable, long-horizon video synthesis from sparse inputs.

### Deep Search in Real-World Web Environments
**153 upvotes** · Xiaomin Yu, Hongliang Chen et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.30181)

Most deep-search agent benchmarks use static retrieval; this paper introduces **OpenDS-World**, 200 tasks that require agents to operate on the live, open Web — real browser interactions, heterogeneous file formats, multi-source evidence synthesis. The best frontier model scores only 39.0% accuracy, while human participants scored just 21.1% across 1,000 attempts, indicating tasks are genuinely hard for both. The gap reveals concrete failure modes: poor tool coordination and weak long-horizon search management. Useful as a realistic stress-test for anyone building research agents.

### NVIDIA/OpenShell — Kernel-enforced sandbox runtime for autonomous AI agents
**893 stars/day** · [Repo](https://github.com/NVIDIA/OpenShell)

Written in Rust (Apache 2.0), OpenShell (v0.1.x, 19,122 stars) is NVIDIA's open runtime for running fleets of AI agents with policy-enforced isolation: kernel-level syscall filtering, per-agent file and network access controls, and formal verification of policy changes before they're applied. Agents can install packages, call APIs, and use credentials within declared bounds — without touching anything outside their sandbox. Directly addresses the production safety gap for agentic workloads, especially relevant as multi-agent orchestration becomes mainstream.
