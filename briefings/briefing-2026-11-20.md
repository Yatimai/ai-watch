### Mind2Act World: A Diagnostic Benchmark for Embodied Mind, Physical Action, and Mind-Action Composition in Robotic Manipulation
**45 upvotes** · Yuzhi Huang et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.26444)

Mind2Act World is a benchmark of 180 simulated robot manipulation configurations (30 tasks × 3 difficulty levels × 2 scene settings) designed to separately measure cognitive reasoning, precise physical execution, and their combination in a closed-loop setting. The key finding is a dramatic compositionality gap: even the best evaluated agent scores 52.7% on reasoning-only tasks and 39.0% on execution-only tasks, but collapses to just 12.0% when both are required together. This reveals that benchmarks which test reasoning and execution in isolation are fundamentally insufficient—a robot can be good at both and still fail catastrophically when it needs to chain them. The benchmark covers multimodal agents, VLAs, and world-action models (WAMs), providing per-system "profile cards" attributing failures to Mind, Act, or composition.

### WeVisForensics: Self-Evolving Agent for Omni AI-Generated Video Detection
**45 upvotes** · Yipeng Du et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.21373)

WeVisForensics is an agentic framework for detecting AI-generated videos using a main investigator agent that dynamically orchestrates a self-evolving library of forensic sub-agents covering both visual and audio artifacts. Its dual-timescale policy optimization (DTPO) simultaneously fast-trains the investigator via RL and slower-refines the expert inspectors using annotated artifact intervals — enabling detection of subtle, localized forgeries that single-pass chain-of-thought methods miss. The authors also release ForenCue-30K, a dataset of role-specific forensic annotations and agent interaction trajectories. The system achieves 95.2% average detection accuracy across in-domain, out-of-domain, and agentic benchmarks, +9.0 percentage points above the prior state of the art.

### WeMM-Video: Pushing the Frontiers of Open-Source Omni-Modal Video Understanding
**39 upvotes** · Changli Tang et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.26409)

WeMM-Video is an open-source family of Chinese-English bilingual models (2B, 4B, 9B, 35B-A3B) that jointly processes text, image, audio, and video, supporting both a fast instruct mode and an explicit step-by-step thinking mode. Trained on ~1.2 trillion tokens with a multi-stage pipeline including multi-teacher distillation and specialist RL, the flagship 35B-A3B model beats proprietary models like Qwen3.5-Omni-Flash and Seed2.0-Mini-0428 on omni-modal video benchmarks, closing the gap with Gemini-3.0-Flash. Notably, the compact 9B variant outperforms the larger open-source Qwen3-Omni-30B-A3B-Instruct. All models, evaluation code, and reproduction pipelines are publicly released.

### Token fraud is here and it's BAD
2026-11-20 · [Post](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/20/token-fraud-is-here-and-its-bad/) · tags: `youtube`, `ai`, `generative-ai`, `llms`, `ai-misuse`

Claire Vo (ChatPRD) documents a wave of organized token fraud against her AI platform: coordinated abuse of free trials and promo codes to harvest LLM tokens for resale or to run commercial services (including Roblox/Fortnite map studios) on top of her agent, without paying. One telling detail: scammers used SVG-of-a-pelican-riding-a-bicycle prompts as a model fingerprinting technique to identify which underlying LLM they were accessing. The takeaway for anyone shipping AI products with free tiers: token abuse at scale is now a real operational threat, not a theoretical one.

### PostgreSQL 19 Released
2026-11-19 · [Post](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/19/postgresql-19/) · tags: `postgresql`, `replication`, `scaling`, `ai-security-research`

PostgreSQL 19 shipped two months later than its usual September cadence, delayed partly by the volume of AI-reported vulnerability disclosures the team had to triage. The headline new feature is the `WAIT FOR LSN` command, which enables "read-your-writes" consistency when routing reads to standbys: a session can block until a replica has replayed the WAL up to a specific log sequence number before querying it, with an optional timeout. This solves a classic distributed-reads consistency headache that Wikipedia has historically solved with a bespoke MySQL shim, now available natively in Postgres.
