## 🔬 Research

### Route Anything: A Study of Model and Policy Routing for General-Purpose AI Systems
**89 upvotes** · Zhou, Ma, Wang et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.05275)

Rather than training a single model to do everything, Route Anything proposes a unified framework for dynamically routing tasks to the best-fit specialized model, tool, or policy in a heterogeneous ecosystem. The authors built a massive benchmark: 374,401 scored runs across 452 candidate configurations spanning 25 benchmarks (language, image, video, speech, code, robotics). Key results: a learned router outperforms the best single fixed model by up to 2.1 points on video generation and achieves 8.82× average efficiency gains — a compelling quantitative case that orchestration beats monolithic scaling.

### Think Less, Locally: Local Preference Learning for Reasoning Compression
**63 upvotes** · Yuan, Xu, Zhang et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.04814)

LoCo targets a concrete inefficiency in large reasoning models: they keep "thinking" past the point where they already have the answer. Instead of globally penalizing long chains-of-thought, LoCo uses DPO to train the model to recognize *state-level* stopping points, generating shorter continuations from the same reasoning prefix. It cuts completion length by 41–45% on math and 23–36% on code with no accuracy loss, and the math-trained model transfers to code benchmarks — suggesting the skill of "knowing when to stop" generalizes. Code and model will be released at [github.com/MuyaoYuan/LoCo](https://github.com/MuyaoYuan/LoCo).

### Words Caught in the Heart: Simulating Person-Specific Expression for Self-Opaque Clients in Psychotherapy
**63 upvotes** · Xiao, Chen, Kuang et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.00617)

Pandora is a psychotherapy client simulator that goes beyond "give the LLM a case description" — it dynamically updates the simulated client's psychological and relational state in response to therapist interventions and uses a separate expression planner to mirror the individual's real language patterns. This matters because existing simulators produce responses that are unrealistically clear and self-aware, making them poor training proxies. Evaluated on 115 real cases, Pandora was judged "AI-like" in only 7.2% of ratings, versus 43–67% for baselines — a substantial step toward realistic clinical training data.

## 🛠 Tools

### vercel-labs/agent-desktop — Computer use for AI agents via the OS accessibility tree
**2,463 stars/day** · Rust · [Repo](https://github.com/vercel-labs/agent-desktop)

Rather than using screenshots and pixel-matching (the OpenAI/Anthropic "computer use" approach), agent-desktop reads the macOS/Windows/Linux **accessibility tree** and returns it as structured JSON with actionable element refs. The agent clicks or types on a ref, re-reads the tree, and loops — no vision model required. It ships as a CLI and a C library (npm: `agent-desktop`), has 14k total stars, and is Apache-2.0 licensed. A leaner, more deterministic alternative to screenshot-based computer use.

### NVIDIA/auto-ontology — Graph-based semantic reasoning layer for LLM/agent workflows
**2,053 stars/day** · Python · [Repo](https://github.com/NVIDIA/auto-ontology)

Auto Ontology sits between raw data and an LLM, building a semantic knowledge graph to enable deterministic query generation and more explainable context retrieval — a structured alternative to pure vector-search RAG. The focus on determinism and explainability is a direct response to reliability concerns in production agent pipelines. NVIDIA-backed provenance adds credibility for enterprise use cases.

## 📡 Analysis

### Qwen 3.8 27B on my laptop can interpret model weights, too
*2026-11-03* · [Post](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/3/qwen-can-interpret-model-weights/) · tags: `local-llms`, `llms`, `qwen`, `paper-review`

Simon digs into the "Sparks of Weight-Space Programming" paper, which showed that GPT-6 Astra can read raw neural network weights as floating-point text and both interpret what a network does and edit it — fixing a reasoning-loop bug in Qwen3.5-0.8B that raised MATH-500 from 7.9% to 48.1% by modifying just 0.016% of weights. The key practical finding Simon adds: **Qwen 3.8 27B running locally** can replicate the weight-interpretation tasks from the paper, not just frontier API models. He also flags that the paper conflates "weights as text" (no tools) with "LLM-guided gradient-free optimization" (with code), a distinction worth keeping in mind when reading the claims.

### I discovered a sound that leaks your exact location (and it's everywhere) — YouTube
*2026-11-03* · [Post](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/3/a-sound-that-leaks-your-exact-location/) · tags: `privacy`, `youtube`

OSINT researcher Colsto demonstrates a technique for geolocating a video purely from near-inaudible aircraft background noise: extract plane sounds, reconstruct Doppler curves, cross-reference against public ADS-B flight data. No metadata, no landmarks required. While not an AI paper per se, it's directly relevant to AI engineers building multimodal pipelines or working on data privacy — audio tracks in training corpora or user-uploaded content may contain precise location signals that are very hard to strip.
