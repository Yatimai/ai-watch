## 🔬 Research

### BizBench: Unveiling Hidden Challenges in Deploying Long-Horizon Agents for Real-World Business
**192 upvotes** · Enhan Zhao et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.20424)

BizBench is a new benchmark for evaluating long-horizon agents in a real payments-company environment, combining 789 policy documents, 601 business tools, and 50 multi-step tasks with hard constraints (resource limits, temporal dependencies, conflicting policies). The headline result: even top frontier models fall short — Claude Opus 5 and GPT-6 Sol hit only 64% and 61.3% **strict** success (goal met + valid execution), while outcome-only metrics can be 10+ points more flattering. A particularly telling signal: DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash averages 191 tool calls per run, ~70% more than the reference, exposing severe efficiency gaps that raw success rates hide.

### Has GPT-6-Astra Achieved Embodied Intelligence?
**156 upvotes** · SenseNova Joint Research Team · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.22471)

This report stress-tests GPT-6-Astra across 12 embodied-AI benchmarks (tabletop manipulation, humanoids, mobile platforms, dexterous hands) and finds it achieves unprecedented scores when given in-context demonstrations — effectively challenging the "one model per task" paradigm. However, real-world deployment on 8 robot configurations exposes hard limits: collecting robot demos for ICL is expensive, and you can't simply buy higher success rates with longer execution time when physical constraints apply. The authors conclude GPT-6-Astra has crossed "embodied competence" but not yet "practical embodied intelligence," and propose a new benchmark generation grounded in real-world needs with a real-time world clock.

### Learning to Think and Explore in 3D World (ACT3D)
**141 upvotes** · Yijiang Li, Bingyang Wang, Nuno Vasconcelos · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.21783)

ACT3D teaches a VLM (Qwen-based) to actively navigate a 3D environment — one frame at a time, like a human — using reinforcement learning driven purely by final-answer correctness, with no supervision over which viewpoints to visit. Across 6 benchmarks, it averages 53.2% accuracy, a +22% gain over the base Qwen model and above proprietary Gemini 3.6 Flash, while using 50–70% fewer input frames than competing open-source 3D methods. Crucially, the RL policy learns to be adaptive: questions answerable from the initial view get only a +0.6% boost, while those requiring exploration get +16.3%, proving the model learns *when* to explore, not just *how*.

## 🛠 Tools

### MadsLorentzen/ai-job-search — Local AI-powered job application framework
**3,860 stars/day** · [Repo](https://github.com/MadsLorentzen/ai-job-search)

A Python framework built on Claude Code that automates the full job application cycle: scraping postings, evaluating fit, tailoring CVs, writing cover letters, and prepping for interviews — all running locally on your own machine. The author is a geophysicist who built it after a layoff and validated it personally: 69 tailored applications → 20 first interviews → 1 signed contract. The project has 55k+ total stars and is entirely independent (no token/crypto affiliation).

### stablyai/orca — Multi-agent coding orchestrator
**1,591 stars/day** · [Repo](https://github.com/stablyai/orca)

Orca (97k+ stars, MIT) is a cross-platform desktop/mobile app that lets you run multiple coding agents (Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Pi) in parallel, each in its own Git worktree, with a unified dashboard. The mobile companion lets you monitor and steer agents from your phone and get notified when they finish or are blocked. It's aimed at developers who want to parallelize AI coding work without juggling multiple terminal sessions.

### Inline rendering with document.currentScript
*2026-10-17* · [Post](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Oct/17/document-currentscript/) · tags: `javascript`

A technique by Lean Rada using `document.currentScript` to make inline `<script>` tags replace themselves with computed values at page load, effectively creating a zero-dependency micro-template engine (`$=(...n)=>document.currentScript.replaceWith(...n)`). Tangentially relevant for AI engineers building lightweight HTML demos or data dashboards without a JS framework.
