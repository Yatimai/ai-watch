## 🔬 Research

### The Trinity: Capability-Driven Recursive Self-Improvement for Multimodal Agents
**301 upvotes** · Weidong Tang et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.36077)

The Trinity is a recursive self-improvement framework for multimodal agents built around three roles — Creator, Explorer, and Evolver — that jointly generate environments and evolve task-solving harnesses without requiring manual iteration. The key insight is that tasks of different modalities can share underlying capability requirements, allowing the system to reorganize a surface-level task tree into a hierarchical capability tree and learn reusable modules. A Capability-Aware Harness Router then selects the right module combination at inference time. The result: +34 points average across 28 training benchmarks and +26 points across 18 unseen benchmarks, including two real-world embodied tasks.

### ChronoReplay: One Answer Interface Is Not Enough for Streaming Spatial Intelligence
**216 upvotes** · Yihao Hu et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.34283)

ChronoReplay addresses "interface lock-in" in spatial VLMs — the tendency for a model trained on one type of spatial readout (e.g., categorical left/right) to degrade on others (e.g., metric distance). The fix is surprisingly structural: replay heterogeneous spatial supervision as chronologically ordered episodes, preserving source-native targets and balancing supervision across task families. A single checkpoint trained this way simultaneously improves OVO-S-Bench, OST-Bench (+13.41 pts), and UCS-Bench. Chronological ordering alone beats a shuffled-order control by ~2.41 OST points, suggesting episode sequence is a meaningful training signal for spatial understanding.

### PDRVLA: A Vision-Language-Action Model with Physical Dynamics Representations
**136 upvotes** · Hanqing Wang et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.37381)

VLA models typically lack physical intuition because they don't model scene dynamics — and using full video generation to compensate adds unacceptable latency. PDRVLA inserts a lightweight Physical Dynamics Module that extracts "physical tokens" (future RGB, depth, and optical-flow features) from vision-language inputs to condition action generation, supervised by a frozen pretrained video world model, with no online video generation at runtime. It achieves 99.3% on LIBERO, 89.9% on LIBERO-Plus, 93.6% on RoboTwin 2.0, and 81.5% on SimplerEnv — strong results, trained entirely on publicly available data augmented with human-to-robot synthesis and embodied chain-of-thought annotation.

### Nutlope/hallmark — Anti-AI-slop design skill for AI coding agents
**2,109 stars/day** · 40,104 total stars · [Repo](https://github.com/Nutlope/hallmark)

Hallmark (by Together AI) is a design system packaged as a "skill" for Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, and OpenCode that explicitly fights the visual tells of AI-generated UIs: eyebrow headings, tracked-capital labels, broken mobile layouts, and empty-frame heroes. It ships 50 material-based themes, runs automated lint checks in a real browser at three gate levels (Floor / Reflex / Finish), and includes a `/hallmark redesign` command that rebuilds existing pages. For engineers shipping front-end with AI agents, this is directly actionable.
