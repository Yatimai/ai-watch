### Antom 3-in-1 Transformer: A Payment Foundation Model
**99 upvotes** · Weiyu Han et al. (Ant International) · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.25403)

A ~7B-parameter foundation model (1.7B activated per task route) that unifies sequence, tabular, and graph representations for payment fraud detection. Pretrained on Ant International's billion-scale transaction data, it cuts overall loss rate by **37.88%** vs. XGBoost online and achieves full-label performance using only 25% of labeled data. This is a rare public report of a production-grade LLM applied to financial risk at scale, with hard business metrics to back it up.

### Lazy Agents Answer Against Their Own Beliefs: RLVB
**86 upvotes** · Richmond Sin Jing Xuan et al. (NTU) · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.32884)

LLM agents in long-horizon tasks often commit to an answer prematurely — even when their own stated belief still lists multiple candidates. RLVB (Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Beliefs) adds a reward signal derived from the simulator's exact posterior at each step, nudging the agent to keep exploring when evidence is insufficient. Across 3 VLMs × 3 benchmarks, RLVB improves over the base model by **+17.1% on average**, outperforms PPO+LLM-judge by **+9.3%** while using **1.6× fewer GPU-hours**, and raises the rate of evidence-sufficient commits from as low as 0.4% to 12%.

### Rewirable Circuits in Transformers with Loom Attention
**85 upvotes** · Da Xiao et al. (Caiyun AI) · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.30634) · [Code](https://github.com/Caiyun-AI/LoomFormer)

Loom Attention lets a Transformer dynamically rewire which earlier head outputs feed into a head's Q/K/V at every token, via a vertical attention over a secondary matrix stream. The resulting LoomFormer dual-stream architecture adds only **2% extra FLOPs** yet, trained on 300B tokens, LoomPythia-2.8B matches Pythia-12B on 5-shot benchmarks — effectively matching models trained with **3× the compute**. This is a credible architectural challenger to standard residual-stream designs.

### GPT 6.2 Astra Solves a Historical Mystery
2026-11-24 · [Post](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/24/astra-historical-mystery/) · tags: `ai`, `llms`, `ai-assisted-search`, `history`, `gpt-6-astra`

GPT-6.2 Astra autonomously identified the creator of an anonymous 1808 sketchbook held at Yale's Beinecke Library in **22 minutes**, using publicly available sources and predicting that a physical archive held at Cleveland State University would confirm its hypothesis — which it did. History professor Mark Humphries draws a measured "Deep Blue moment" analogy: unlike math, history is interpretive and humanistic, so the existential crisis for the discipline will likely be milder — though he expects a confidence crisis by spring/summer 2027.
