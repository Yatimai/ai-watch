## 🔬 Research

### Beyond Teacher Assignment: Domain-Normalized Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation
**114 upvotes** · Xin Li et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.35347)

Multi-teacher on-policy distillation (MOPD) merges specialist LLMs (math, coding, instruction-following) into a single student model by routing each prompt to the right expert — but routing alone isn't enough. The authors show that instruction-following feedback has far greater variance than math feedback, effectively drowning out the math specialist's signal. Their fix, **DN-MOPD**, rescales each domain's gradient by its measured spread, recovering most of the lost math gain across all three Qwen3.5 model sizes and six benchmarks. The key practical takeaway: when merging specialists, *weighting* teacher feedback by domain is at least as important as *selecting* which teacher to use.

### Post-Training Leaves Behavioral Shadows on Unrelated Decisions
**86 upvotes** · Ziyang Zhang et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.29233)

**Active Taskless Distillation (ATD)** demonstrates that a student model can absorb a teacher's post-training capabilities from as little as **one word per prompt** — no task examples, no logits, no teacher weights. By finding prompts where both models are nearly indifferent between two ordinary words and training only on the teacher's choice, the student achieves a **+5.34 pp gain on HumanEval+** with Qwen2.5-1.5B. This has significant implications for model security and data privacy: fine-tuning signals can leak through seemingly innocuous outputs, and the effect generalizes across model families and domains (coding, science, reasoning).

### Duplex-MPE: Benchmarking Multi-Party Interaction in Full-Duplex Dialogue
**65 upvotes** · Chengqian Ma et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.31948)

Existing full-duplex speech benchmarks focus on two-party turn-taking; **Duplex-MPE** fills the gap with 2,000 multi-party scenarios (3–4 humans + 1 assistant) testing whether a speech model should respond, stay silent, or stop mid-sentence. Five open-weight systems are evaluated (MiniCPM-o 4.5, Moshi, FLM-Audio, Voila, Freeze-Omni); MiniCPM-o 4.5 leads on three of four metrics, but all speech models struggle to distinguish explicit vs. implicit addressing — a gap where a text-based Gemini 3.1 Pro reference shows a **64.3 pp difference**. A useful resource for anyone building voice assistants meant to operate in real meeting or group-conversation contexts.

### vectorize-io/hindsight — Agent memory that learns, not just remembers
**2 541 stars/day** · ⭐ 42 128 total · [Repo](https://github.com/vectorize-io/hindsight)

Hindsight is an MIT-licensed Python library providing an adaptive memory layer for AI agents: rather than simply replaying conversation history, it extracts and evolves behavioral rules from past interactions so agents improve over time. It ships with a benchmarking suite, a managed cloud option, and Python/NPM clients. At 42 k stars and backed by an arXiv paper, it has become a reference implementation for long-term agent learning.
