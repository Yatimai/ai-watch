### WeVisForensics: Self-Evolving Agent for Omni AI-Generated Video Detection
**56 upvotes** · Yipeng Du et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.21373)

WeVisForensics is an agentic framework that coordinates a main investigator agent with a self-evolving library of forensic sub-agents covering both visual and audio cues to detect AI-generated videos. Its dual-timescale policy optimization (DTPO) uses reinforcement learning to simultaneously improve the investigation strategy and the individual inspectors. The system is trained on ForenCue-30K, a new dataset of 30K annotated forensic trajectories. It achieves 95.2% detection accuracy across in-domain and out-of-domain benchmarks, 9 points above the prior state of the art — a significant leap as deepfake video generation becomes commoditized.

### Mind2Act World: A Diagnostic Benchmark for Embodied Mind, Physical Action, and Mind-Action Composition in Robotic Manipulation
**53 upvotes** · Yuzhi Huang et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.26444)

Mind2Act World is a 180-configuration benchmark across 30 simulated manipulation tasks designed to separately and jointly evaluate cognitive reasoning (Mind), physical execution (Act), and their composition (Mind2Act) in robots. The key finding is stark: even the strongest evaluated agent scores 52.7% on Mind tasks and 39.0% on Act tasks, but collapses to just 12.0% on combined Mind2Act tasks. This reveals that passing individual capability tests does not guarantee their composition under real closed-loop conditions — a critical gap for robotics practitioners relying on current VLA and world-action models.

### WeMM-Video: Pushing the Frontiers of Open-Source Omni-Modal Video Understanding
**45 upvotes** · Changli Tang et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.26409)

WeMM-Video is a family of open-source bilingual (Chinese/English) omni-modal models (2B, 4B, 9B, 35B-A3B) trained on ~1.2 trillion tokens, unifying text, image, audio, and video understanding with both a fast instruct mode and a step-by-step thinking mode. The flagship 35B-A3B beats proprietary models like Qwen3.5-Omni-Flash and Seed2.0-Mini, and the compact 9B outperforms the larger Qwen3-Omni-30B-A3B-Instruct. Evaluated across 120+ benchmarks and fully open-sourced with the evaluation pipeline, this is a meaningful step toward closing the gap between open and closed models on audio-video reasoning tasks.
