## 🔬 Research

### The Trinity: Capability-Driven Recursive Self-Improvement for Multimodal Agents
**541 upvotes** · Weidong Tang et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.36077)

The Trinity is a recursive self-improvement framework for multimodal agents that decouples task *surface form* from underlying *capability requirements*, reorganizing tasks into a hierarchical capability tree. Three roles — Creator, Explorer, Evolver — jointly generate simulated training environments and evolve reusable "Harness" modules without requiring manual iteration. A Capability-Aware Harness Router then selects the right module configuration at inference time. The evolved Harnesses improve mean performance by **+34 points** across 28 training benchmarks and **+26 points** across 18 unseen benchmarks, including two real-world embodied tasks.

### Multiscale Policy Optimization for Long-Horizon LLM Agents
**378 upvotes** · Tao Ren et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.35013)

Standard RL for LLM agents assigns credit token-by-token, but critic values actually vary at the *turn* level — each full assistant response. MPO introduces Multiscale Advantage Estimation (MAE) combined with turn-level importance sampling to align credit assignment and policy updates at the right granularity. Tested on search, tool-use, and automation tasks with 4B and 30B-A3B models, it consistently outperforms strong RL baselines. Practically relevant for anyone training agentic models on long multi-turn trajectories.

### DTCA: Denoising Token Credit Assignment for RL in Diffusion Language Models
**256 upvotes** · Hongze Chen et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.35201)

Diffusion Language Models (DLMs) generate text through iterative masked denoising rather than left-to-right decoding, making standard RL credit assignment poorly suited — a single outcome reward says little about which token commitments mattered. DTCA estimates each token's contribution by measuring the improvement in prediction confidence of remaining masked positions after that token is committed, then groups signals by part-of-speech category and calibrates them via token-level entropy changes. On LLaDA-8B-Instruct, DTCA yields **+9.0 points on MATH500** and **+5.3 points on GSM8K** over the base model, a meaningful step toward making DLMs competitive with autoregressive models on reasoning tasks.

## 🛠 Tools

### pbakaus/impeccable — Design vocabulary for coding agents
**2,881 stars/day** · ⭐ 94,431 total · Rust · [Repo](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable)

Impeccable is a set of slash-command "design primitives" (`/polish`, `/bolder`, `/critique`, `/audit`) that you install into your coding agent (Claude Code, Cursor, Codex CLI, Copilot, Devin, Gemini CLI, and ~15 others) to steer UI output away from generic AI defaults — the ubiquitous Inter font, purple-to-blue gradients, and nested card layouts. Install is a single `npx impeccable install`. The before/after demo in the README is striking: same model, same brief, dramatically different visual result — making it a practical antidote to "AI slop" UIs before they ship.

**1,926 stars/day** · ⭐ 49,224 total · Python · [Repo](https://github.com/HKUDS/DeepTutor)
