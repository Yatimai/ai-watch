## 🔬 Research

### VFlash: Fast, Accurate Visual Planning via Flash Primitive Decoding
**98 upvotes** · Yu-Cheng Chou et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.08449)

Autoregressive VLMs generate spatial primitives (points, boxes, trajectories) one token at a time, making visual planning expensive at inference. VFlash introduces a lightweight "drafter" that predicts an entire primitive—boxes or variable-length trajectories—in a single forward pass, bypassing strict token-sequence agreement. The result: ~25× faster generation on 3D point motion forecasting, and a world action model conditioned on VFlash plans hits 70.2% average success across four MolmoSpaces suites vs. 62.6% for the autoregressive baseline. Directly relevant for anyone building real-time robotics or autonomous driving pipelines.

### Decoupled Predictive Decision Making (DPDM): A Shared Conditional Predictor for Agents
**79 upvotes** · Lang Mo et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.02075)

DPDM separates semantic understanding (handled by a frozen LLM encoder) from empirical inference (handled by a frozen structured-data predictor, LimiX-2), letting agents learn from deployment history without fine-tuning either model. A single predictor instance covers tool selection, candidate ranking, and reasoning assessment across 50 benchmarks, raising mean accuracy from 78.80% (few-shot) to 84.92%. On ToolPRMBench tasks (BFCL & ToolSandbox), absolute gains reach +34.26% and +28.87%. The key insight is that "learning from experience" becomes a modular, independently improvable capability rather than something baked into one monolithic model.

### SLA: Scaling Spatial-Language-Action Model via Continual Pre-training
**77 upvotes** · Fuhao Li et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.08373)

Most VLA models inherit 2D visual representations and only add spatial supervision at fine-tuning time, leaving 3D understanding shallow. SLA builds on a pretrained pi05 policy and jointly optimizes action prediction with alignment to a frozen 3D perception teacher throughout continual pre-training, using feature-dependent gates to aggregate multi-layer visual embeddings. It handles heterogeneous datasets with partial depth and camera calibration, and uses probabilistic state dropout to prevent overfitting to demonstration trajectories. All auxiliary components are discarded at inference, so the original VLA weights and architecture are preserved with no deployment overhead.

## 🛠 Tools

### cloudflare/security-audit-skill — Multi-phase AI security auditor
**2088 stars/day** · [Repo](https://github.com/cloudflare/security-audit-skill)

This is the open-source seed of Cloudflare's internal vulnerability discovery harness. It orchestrates isolated AI agents through six structured phases—recon, coverage-led hunting, candidate validation, structured output, independent verification, and neutral reporting—with machine-readable outputs (`findings.json`, `REPORT.md`) validated against a JSON schema. Multiple runs against the same repo are additive and results are deduplicated, making it suited for CI-integrated security workflows. With 64k+ total stars, it's already one of the most adopted coding-agent skills in the security space.

---

### milind-soni/OpenMausBot — Open-source local-first AI bot platform
**1986 stars/day** · [Repo](https://github.com/milind-soni/OpenMausBot)

OpenMausBot is a bring-your-own-agent desktop app (macOS/Windows/Ubuntu via Electron) positioning itself as an open-source alternative to Grok Bot, Meta Muse, and OpenAI Dots. Each "bot" in the sidebar is an independent agent (Claude or Codex) with its own model, personality, and sandboxed cloud VM. The local-first architecture means you control which models run and can inspect agent actions before approving them—a meaningful differentiator in a space where most competitors are closed SaaS.

**1111 stars/day** · [Repo](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills)
