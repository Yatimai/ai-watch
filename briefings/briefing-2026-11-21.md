### WeVisForensics: Self-Evolving Agent for Omni AI-Generated Video Detection
**52 upvotes** · Yipeng Du et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.21373)

WeVisForensics is an agentic framework for detecting AI-generated videos, where a main investigator agent coordinates a self-evolving library of forensic sub-agents covering both visual and audio cues. The key innovation is **DTPO** (Dual-Timescale Policy Optimization): the investigator is fine-tuned quickly via RL while the inspector sub-agents are refined more slowly, guided by annotated artifact timestamps. The system also ships **ForenCue-30K**, a new dataset with role-specific forensic annotations. It achieves 95.2% average detection accuracy across in-domain, out-of-domain, and agentic benchmarks — a **+9.0 pp** gain over the prior best detector.

### Mind2Act World: A Diagnostic Benchmark for Embodied Mind, Physical Action, and Mind-Action Composition in Robotic Manipulation
**50 upvotes** · Yuzhi Huang et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.26444)

Mind2Act World is a 30-task simulated robotic manipulation benchmark designed to separately diagnose reasoning, execution, and their composition. The 180 configurations are split across three views: *Mind* (history-based decisions), *Act* (precise physical execution), and *Mind2Act* (closed-loop combination). The results are sobering: even the strongest agent scores 52.7% on Mind tasks and 39.0% on Act tasks but collapses to **12.0%** when both must be composed. This clearly isolates composing reasoning with execution via environmental feedback as the central unsolved problem for robotics.

### Probing the "Conscious" Mind: Selective Access and Global Workspace in Vision-Language Models
**41 upvotes** · Jiahao Huang, Jun Wang · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.26590)

This paper applies **Global Workspace Theory** (a neuroscience framework for consciousness) to vision-language models using a new tool called the **Multimodal Concept Lens (MC-Lens)**, a Jacobian-based probe that maps internal representations to a shared semantic space across both vision and language modalities. Key findings: multimodal concept information is selectively accessible depending on task demands, accessed concepts causally influence downstream reasoning, and reliable selective access emerges *later* in training than basic concept accessibility. These patterns are consistent across VLM families and sizes, offering a principled lens for diagnosing and improving cross-modal alignment.

### Token fraud is here and it's BAD — Free-trial abuse draining AI platform revenue
2026-11-20 · [Post](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/20/token-fraud-is-here-and-its-bad/) · tags: `ai-misuse`, `llms`, `generative-ai`

Claire Vo (ChatPRD) documents a systematic wave of token fraud against her AI platform: coordinated groups loop free trials and promo codes to tunnel into her agent for free LLM usage, then resell that capacity or use it to run commercial operations (e.g. one group was generating Roblox/Fortnite maps). A tell-tale sign: fraudsters probe the underlying model by prompting for SVGs of *pelicans riding bicycles*, a known fingerprinting trick used to identify models by their stylistic signatures. Claire is shutting down free trials entirely — a cautionary tale for any AI product team offering generous trial access.
