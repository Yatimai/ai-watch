## 🔬 Research

### cmHC: Balancing Stream Mixing in Hyper-Connections via Geometric Contraction
**85 upvotes** · He et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.27193)

Hyper-Connections allow LLMs to route information across multiple residual streams rather than a single one, but naïve mixing either leaves streams too independent (under-mixed) or indistinguishably similar (over-mixed). cmHC solves this by geometrically contracting the Birkhoff polytope — the space of valid mixing matrices — toward its interior, enforcing a provably balanced mixing without adding parameters. Applied to both dense and MoE models at large scale, it also ships fused Ascend NPU operators that cut hyper-connection latency by up to **44.8%**, making it practically relevant for large-scale pretraining infra teams.

### Mind2Act World: A Diagnostic Benchmark for Embodied Mind, Physical Action, and Mind-Action Composition in Robotic Manipulation
**57 upvotes** · Huang et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.26444)

Current robotics benchmarks conflate reasoning and execution failures, making it impossible to pinpoint *why* a system fails. Mind2Act World disentangles this with 30 manipulation tasks × 3 difficulty levels × 2 scene settings (180 configs total), split into three views: **Mind** (reasoning from history), **Act** (precise execution), and **Mind2Act** (closed-loop composition). The key finding is stark: the best evaluated agent scores 52.7% on Mind and 39.0% on Act, but only **12.0% on combined Mind2Act tasks** — composing both capabilities through environmental feedback remains the central unsolved problem in embodied AI.

### Non-penetrating World Models
**54 upvotes** · Dai et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.27963)

Open-source video world models currently let simulated objects pass through physical obstacles **74% of the time** on average — a critical failure for robotics and game simulation use cases. The key insight here is that DiT hidden states already encode enough information to predict whether an action will cause penetration; a lightweight prediction head trained on those frozen states can flag unsafe actions and reroute them. The resulting framework, **NoPeWorld**, drops the violation rate from 74% to **16%** across six models, with zero-shot generalization to open-domain imagery and moving obstacles. Code, benchmark (NoPeBench), and weights are publicly released at [nopeworld.github.io](https://nopeworld.github.io).
