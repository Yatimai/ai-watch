### Stable Gated DeltaNet-2: Understanding and Mitigating Recurrent State Instability
**125 upvotes** · Zhang et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.34051)

Gated DeltaNet-2 (GDN-2) improved linear attention with fine-grained channel-wise control over state reads/writes, but hit training instabilities at long contexts due to asymmetric erase operators causing exponential state growth. This paper diagnoses the root cause and proposes a fix — symmetric erasure + decay-regulated residual writing — with a provable O(T) state growth bound. On ~1.6B parameter models, the fix yields +6.80 points on the 128K RULER benchmark (recurrent setting) and +0.62 points average on 9 downstream tasks (hybrid setting), with stable training throughout. Relevant for anyone building or fine-tuning linear/subquadratic attention models on long contexts.

### OS-Omni: Benchmarking Generalist Computer-Using Agents on Long-Horizon Workflows Across Platforms
**99 upvotes** · Shen et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.34232)

OS-Omni is a live cross-platform benchmark covering Windows, Ubuntu, macOS, iOS, Android, and Web, designed for long-horizon agentic tasks (avg. 67.54 interaction steps vs. 15.0 in OSWorld). The best current agent, Claude Opus 5.5, scores 68.5% — 8.8 points behind the human reference of 77.3% — with the gap exceeding 25 points on iOS and macOS specifically. The benchmark includes reproducible initialization, persistent-state applications (cart, calendar, budget, etc.), and versioned task releases of 300 tasks per wave. This is a meaningful stress-test for anyone building or evaluating computer-use agents in production.

### Mixture-of-Loops Transformer (MoLT)
**73 upvotes** · Zhu et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.34923) · [Code](https://github.com/hustvl/MoLT)

MoLT rethinks Looped Transformers by using keys and values (KV) as cross-loop memory, introducing two variants: MoLT-R (retains residual stream for expressivity) and MoLT-KV (restarts from embeddings for efficiency, enabling single-pass decoding). The U-shaped variant (U-MoLT) achieves 1.7× validation cross-entropy token efficiency at 700M parameters compared to OLMo3, and matches non-looped OLMo3 inference latency (996.6 vs. 1,000.6 ms on ~113M models). Continued pretraining of Qwen3.5-0.8B-Base with U-MoLT lowers cross-entropy on all 11 domains with 1.4× mean token efficiency — a compelling efficiency-expressivity tradeoff for practitioners constrained by compute budgets.

### sqlite3_rsync: Database Remote-Copy Tool For SQLite
2026-11-26 · [Post](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/26/sqlite3-rsync/) · tags: `backups`, `sqlite`

`sqlite3_rsync` (shipped experimentally in SQLite 3.47.0, Oct 2024) can copy a live SQLite database snapshot directly over SSH to a remote machine — without first creating a full local copy. This is a critical practical advantage over `VACUUM INTO` or the backup API, which require free disk space equal to the full database size. The protocol uses cryptographic page hashing à la rsync, minimizing retransmitted data on subsequent syncs. Directly relevant for anyone running SQLite-backed apps on small cloud instances.
