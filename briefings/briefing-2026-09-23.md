## 🔬 Research

### Realtime-Venus: A full-duplex interaction system with asynchronous delegation
**201 upvotes** · Ruixiang Zhao et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.13814)

Realtime-Venus is a proactive full-duplex spoken and audio-visual dialogue system built on two separate 9B models (Omni for video+audio, Audio for speech-only). A dual-loop architecture keeps the conversation flowing in the foreground while a background harness asynchronously executes reasoning and tool calls, then reintegrates the results — solving the long-standing latency vs. quality tradeoff in real-time assistants. On audio benchmarks, Realtime-Venus-Audio beats Gemini 3.1 Live and GPT-4o on all three interruption-handling metrics, and Omni tops six of eight video streaming benchmarks. For engineers building voice or video agents, the publicly detailed post-training recipe (offline understanding + duplex trajectories + delegation workflows) is directly actionable.

### OmniEdu: Open Foundation Models for Learning and Teaching
**140 upvotes** · Hao Liang et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.23088)

OmniEdu is an open family of education-focused LLMs (4B, 9B, 27B) fine-tuned on a carefully curated corpus of ~70K examples from 100+ educational sources, organized around four capabilities: subject competence, curriculum grounding, diagnostic reasoning, and pedagogical scaffolding. OmniEdu-27B hits 63.12% EM on K12-Bench and leads LongTutor's teaching average at 3.02 — outperforming general-purpose models at the same scale on education tasks. The open release matters because most competitive edu-AI products are proprietary; this gives researchers and EdTech builders a tunable baseline across K-12 problem solving and tutoring.

### GameHorizon Suite: Multi-Horizon Data and Evaluation in Gameplay
**113 upvotes** · Yiran Wang et al. · [Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.25001)

GameHorizon is a three-part benchmark suite for evaluating AI models on real video game play: an automated annotation pipeline, a 5,000-hour AAA gameplay dataset (21 games, 100 expert players), and an offline+online evaluation bench. Unlike prior datasets, it covers multiple temporal horizons — from short reflexive actions to long-horizon planning — and tests whether offline scores actually predict live in-game performance. With 47 models evaluated over 1M+ invocations, it already reveals sharp capability differences that task-level benchmarks miss, making it a credible new standard for embodied/game agent research.

## 🛠 Tools

### google/ax — Kubernetes-style orchestrator for autonomous agent workloads
**1,542 stars/day** · Go · [Repo](https://github.com/google/ax)

AX lets you declare agentic tasks in YAML (similar to `kubectl apply`) and handles sandbox isolation, workspace pre-wiring (Git repos, MCP servers), network fencing, and cluster-scale scheduling on top of Agent Substrate. It targets the specific failure modes of agents — runaway spending, unbounded network access, stateful accumulation — with four declarative primitives: `Task`, `Workspace`, `Gateway`, and skill packages. Still pre-stable (breaking changes expected), but at 8,354 total stars and 1,500+/day it's clearly generating serious attention from platform engineers thinking about agent infrastructure at scale.

---

### browser-use/video-use — AI-driven video editing via coding agents
**745 stars/day** · Python · [Repo](https://github.com/browser-use/video-use)

video-use lets you drop raw footage in a folder, describe what you want in plain language to Claude Code (or any shell-capable agent), and get back a finished `final.mp4` — handling filler-word cuts, color grading, subtitle burning, and animation overlays via parallel sub-agents. It integrates ffmpeg, ElevenLabs, and frameworks like Remotion and Manim, and self-evaluates rendered output at each cut boundary before surfacing results. With 26,257 total stars it's one of the fastest-growing projects from the browser-use team, and a concrete example of coding agents replacing traditional NLE GUIs for content creators.

### anthropics/financial-services — Reference agents and skills for FSI workflows
**665 stars/day** · Python · [Repo](https://github.com/anthropics/financial-services)

Anthropic's official FSI reference repo ships named end-to-end workflow agents (Pitch Agent, Market Researcher, GL Reconciler, etc.) for investment banking, equity research, private equity, and wealth management — deployable either as Claude Cowork plugins or via the new Claude Managed Agents API. Every agent bundles its own skills and data connectors (DCF, `/comps`, `/earnings`) and stages all outputs for human review, with no autonomous transaction execution. At 36,709 total stars and pushed just 2 days ago, this is Anthropic's clearest signal yet of a push into high-value enterprise verticals.
